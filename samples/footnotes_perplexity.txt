Algeria's startup scene has grown quickly since 2020 [1]. More than 7,800 ventures are registered on startup.dz [1][2], and about 2,300 hold the official label [2].

## Funding
The Algeria Startup Fund holds DZD 2.4 billion [3]. VOLZ raised $5 million in December 2025 [4, 5].
Key steps for founders:
1. Register on startup.dz
2. Apply for the label

Analysts expect more exits in 2026.
