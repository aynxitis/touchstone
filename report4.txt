The European electric vehicle (EV) market experienced robust growth in 2025, with battery-electric vehicles (BEVs) achieving record sales and market penetration across the continent.

### Overall European Sales & Market Share (2025)

* **Total EV Sales:** 2,585,187 battery-electric vehicles were sold across Europe (EU, EFTA, and the UK) in 2025 ([Eleport](https://eleport.com/ev-sales-in-europe/)).
* **Year-Over-Year Growth:** This represents a strong **29.7% increase** compared to the 1,992,803 EVs sold in 2024 ([Eleport](https://eleport.com/ev-sales-in-europe/)).
* **Market Share:** EVs captured **19.5%** of the total European new car market, meaning roughly one in five new cars sold was fully electric ([Eleport](https://eleport.com/ev-sales-in-europe/)).

---

### Sales Figures and Market Share by Country

Adoption rates varied significantly by region, heavily concentrated in Northern and Western Europe.

* **Norway:** 172,231 EVs sold (+50.6%), dominating with a **95.9%** EV market share ([Eleport](https://eleport.com/ev-sales-in-europe/)).
* **Germany:** 545,142 EVs sold (+43.2%), achieving a **20.1%** EV market share. Germany remained the largest European EV market by total volume ([Eleport](https://eleport.com/ev-sales-in-europe/)).
* **United Kingdom:** 473,348 EVs sold (+23.9%) ([Eleport](https://eleport.com/ev-sales-in-europe/)).
* **France:** 326,922 EVs sold (+12.5%), reaching a **20.0%** EV market share ([Eleport](https://eleport.com/ev-sales-in-europe/)).
* **Denmark:** Reached an estimated **68%** EV market share ([Best Selling Cars](https://www.best-selling-cars.com/europe/2025-full-year-europe-bev-electric-car-sales-per-european-country/)).
* **Netherlands:** Reached a **40%** EV market share ([Best Selling Cars](https://www.best-selling-cars.com/europe/2025-full-year-europe-bev-electric-car-sales-per-european-country/)).
* **Finland:** 26,745 EVs sold (+22.3%), resulting in a **37.2%** EV market share ([Eleport](https://eleport.com/ev-sales-in-europe/)).
* **Sweden:** 99,723 EVs sold (+5.7%), achieving a **36.5%** EV market share ([Eleport](https://eleport.com/ev-sales-in-europe/)).
* **Austria:** 60,651 EVs sold (+35.9%), reaching a **21.3%** EV market share ([Eleport](https://eleport.com/ev-sales-in-europe/)).
* **Poland:** 43,311 EVs sold (+161.5%), reaching a **7.2%** EV market share ([Eleport](https://eleport.com/ev-sales-in-europe/)).
* **Spain & Italy:** EV adoption remained much lower, finishing at **9%** and **6%** market share, respectively ([Best Selling Cars](https://www.best-selling-cars.com/europe/2025-full-year-europe-bev-electric-car-sales-per-european-country/)).

### Brand Performance and Top EV Models

While exact percentage shares for every individual brand across the entire continent are highly fragmented, market positioning shifted dramatically among the top automakers:

* **Volkswagen:** Reclaimed the title as the **#1 best-selling electric car brand** in Europe for the full year ([Best Selling Cars](https://www.best-selling-cars.com/europe/2025-full-year-europe-bev-electric-car-sales-per-european-country/)).
* **Tesla:** Despite having the most popular individual car, Tesla struggled as a brand in 2025. Its total European vehicle sales plummeted by **26.9%** to 238,656 total units ([Best Selling Cars](https://www.best-selling-cars.com/europe/2025-full-year-europe-best-selling-car-manufacturers-and-brands/)).
* **BYD:** Experienced massive explosive growth, with its total European sales rocketing by **268.6%** to 187,657 units ([Best Selling Cars](https://www.best-selling-cars.com/europe/2025-full-year-europe-best-selling-car-manufacturers-and-brands/)).
